# Regression: brainstorming should create breadth without another dossier

## Prompt

> The intent brief is approved. Before shaping, give me seven contrasting ways to approach this. Keep the options compact and do not give me a research report.

## Pass conditions

- Six to eight options.
- About 70–140 words each.
- Every option contains only core move, makes easier, main catch, and best fit.
- At least four distinct governing premises.
- Includes a minimal/null option when credible.
- Includes an inversion of authority, ownership, workflow, or requested mechanism when credible.
- Ends with no more than three strongest contrasts and an optional short lean.

## Fail conditions

- Source list, citations, evidence IDs, score matrix, or detailed architecture.
- Seven variations of the same architecture.
- Repeated restatement of the stable intent under every option.
- Selecting a winner before generating independent options.
- More than 1,800 words without explicit user request.
